# Headache Clinical Guidelines (Simplified)

## Common Causes of Headache

Headache is one of the most common symptoms in clinical practice. The main types include:

### 1. Tension-type Headache
- Most common type, accounts for 70% of primary headaches
- Bilateral, pressing or tightening quality
- Mild to moderate intensity
- Not aggravated by routine physical activity
- Treatment: rest, stress management, NSAIDs (ibuprofen 400mg, acetaminophen 500mg)

### 2. Migraine
- Unilateral, pulsating quality
- Moderate to severe intensity
- Aggravated by routine physical activity
- May have aura (visual disturbances)
- Nausea and vomiting common
- Treatment: triptans (sumatriptan 50-100mg), NSAIDs, antiemetics
- Prevention: propranolol, topiramate, amitriptyline

### 3. Cluster Headache
- Severe unilateral orbital/temporal pain
- Duration 15-180 minutes
- Associated autonomic features (tearing, nasal congestion)
- Treatment: oxygen therapy, sumatriptan injection

## Red Flags (Emergency)
- Thunderclap headache (worst headache of life, sudden onset)
- Headache with fever and neck stiffness (meningitis)
- Progressive headache with papilledema (intracranial pressure)
- New headache after age 50
- Headache after head trauma
- Headache with neurological deficits

## When to Refer
- Red flag symptoms present
- Failed conservative treatment > 3 months
- New daily persistent headache
- Medication overuse headache
