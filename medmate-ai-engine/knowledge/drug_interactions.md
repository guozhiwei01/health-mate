# Common Drug Interactions Reference

## NSAIDs (Ibuprofen, Aspirin, Naproxen)
- Avoid with: anticoagulants (warfarin) - increased bleeding risk
- Avoid with: ACE inhibitors/ARBs - reduced antihypertensive effect
- Avoid with: corticosteroids - increased GI bleeding risk
- Avoid with: lithium - increased lithium levels
- Caution with: methotrexate - increased toxicity

## Ibuprofen + Amoxicillin
- Generally SAFE to take together
- No clinically significant interaction
- Ibuprofen for pain/fever, amoxicillin for bacterial infection
- Take ibuprofen with food to reduce GI irritation
- Complete full course of amoxicillin

## Metformin (Diabetes)
- Avoid with: alcohol - increased lactic acidosis risk
- Caution with: contrast dye - temporary discontinuation needed
- Monitor with: ACE inhibitors - may affect kidney function
- Take with meals to reduce GI side effects

## Statins (Atorvastatin, Simvastatin)
- Avoid with: grapefruit juice - increased statin levels
- Avoid with: macrolide antibiotics (erythromycin, clarithromycin)
- Monitor with: warfarin - may increase INR
- Report muscle pain immediately (rhabdomyolysis risk)

## Antihypertensive Combinations
- ACEI + ARB: NOT recommended (increased side effects, no benefit)
- ACEI/ARB + CCB: Good combination
- ACEI/ARB + Diuretic: Good combination
- CCB + Diuretic: Acceptable
- Beta-blocker + CCB (non-dihydropyridine): Caution - risk of bradycardia

## Traditional Chinese Medicine Interactions
- Ginseng: may reduce warfarin effectiveness
- Ginkgo biloba: increased bleeding risk with anticoagulants
- Licorice (Gan Cao): may cause hypokalemia with diuretics
- St. John's Wort: reduces effectiveness of many medications
