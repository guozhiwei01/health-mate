# Hypertension Management Guidelines (Simplified)

## Definition
- Normal: systolic < 120 mmHg AND diastolic < 80 mmHg
- Elevated: systolic 120-129 mmHg AND diastolic < 80 mmHg
- Stage 1 Hypertension: systolic 130-139 OR diastolic 80-89 mmHg
- Stage 2 Hypertension: systolic >= 140 OR diastolic >= 90 mmHg
- Hypertensive Crisis: systolic > 180 OR diastolic > 120 mmHg

## Lifestyle Modifications
- DASH diet: rich in fruits, vegetables, whole grains
- Reduce sodium intake: < 2300 mg/day (ideally < 1500 mg)
- Regular exercise: 150 minutes/week moderate-intensity aerobic
- Weight management: BMI 18.5-24.9
- Limit alcohol: men <= 2 drinks/day, women <= 1 drink/day
- Quit smoking
- Stress management

## First-line Medications
1. ACE Inhibitors (ACEI): enalapril 5-40mg, lisinopril 10-40mg
   - Dry cough side effect (10-15%)
   - Contraindicated in pregnancy
2. ARBs: losartan 50-100mg, valsartan 80-320mg
   - Better tolerated than ACEI
   - Same contraindications
3. CCBs: amlodipine 2.5-10mg, nifedipine 30-60mg
   - Ankle edema common
   - Good for elderly patients
4. Diuretics: hydrochlorothiazide 12.5-25mg
   - Monitor potassium levels
   - May increase blood glucose

## Blood Pressure Monitoring
- Home monitoring: measure twice daily (morning and evening)
- Record: date, time, systolic, diastolic, heart rate
- Target: < 130/80 mmHg for most adults
- Target for elderly (>= 65): < 140/90 mmHg

## Emergency: Hypertensive Crisis
- Systolic > 180 or Diastolic > 120
- With target organ damage: call 120 immediately
- Without organ damage: urgent medical evaluation within hours
